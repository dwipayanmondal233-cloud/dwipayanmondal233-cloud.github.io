Magic wand & image

Magic wand (W): click a color on an image and it turns transparent. Higher tolerance removes more similar colors. Split gap: pieces closer than this stay together.

    Image tools

    1) Select the image, press Remove BG (or use the Magic wand). 2) Press Split parts to separate every piece. For pieces that touch, draw a Pen outline around the part, select it, and press Cut out.

    Align & distribute

    Shift+click selects several shapes. Mouse wheel zooms. Hold Space and drag to pan. Press 0 to reset the view. Ctrl+Shift+V pastes the selection into a new page.

    The orange frame is your canvas. Only what is inside it is exported. JPG cannot be transparent, so it uses white if Background is Transparent. Max size 4096 px.
